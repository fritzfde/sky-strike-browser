SKY STRIKE

Arrow Down = nose up · Arrow Up = nose down · Arrow Left/Right = roll · A/D = rudder (and nosewheel steering)
W/S = throttle 0–100% (it holds) · hold Shift = afterburner (at full throttle)
G = gear · B = brakes · Mouse/touchpad = free look (C = recenter)
Space / left click = guns · F = missile (keep target in the box ~1 s to lock)
Takeoff: B off, full throttle, rotate with Arrow Down at about 250 km/h · Land: gear down, slow and level, on a runway · stop on an airfield to repair and rearm
P / Esc = pause · M = mute

Click / tap to start
