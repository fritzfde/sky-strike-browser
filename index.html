SKY STRIKE v0.6.1

Arrow Down = pull (fly-by-wire: max 9 G / AoA limit, stall only below ~200 kt) · Arrow Up = nose down · Arrow Left/Right = roll · A/D or Q/E = rudder (nosewheel steering on the ground)
W/S = throttle 0–100% (holds, ~0.8 s full travel) · 1–9, 0 = 10–100% · hold Shift = afterburner (at 100%) · engine spools up in ~1.5 s
G = gear (sets flaps HALF if AUTO) · L = flaps AUTO → HALF (takeoff) → FULL (landing, max 250 kt, auto-retracts) · X = speed brake · B = wheel brakes · V = cockpit / chase view · M = map
Mouse / touchpad = free look (C = recenter) · Space / left click = guns · F = missile: 4 × IR (≤1.6 NM) + 2 × radar (≤3.2 NM), lock = hold target in box ~1 s; unlocked = unguided · gun 510 rds · HP regenerates after 5 s, missile hit = 60% · R = flares (30, refill on airfield) — LOCK / MISSILE warning + arrow show the threat · defeat missiles: flares (IR), hard break turn / beam (radar notch) when close, or outrun at long range
Takeoff: B off, full throttle + Shift, Arrow Down at ~120–140 kt, gear up · Land: gear + flaps, ~140 kt, under 1000 ft/min sink, wings level on the runway, then B + X · Slip: aileron one way + opposite rudder (more sink, same speed)
Stall: full aft stick at low speed → buffet, wing drop · Spin recovery (PARE): idle, ailerons neutral, full OPPOSITE rudder, stick forward, then pull out gently
Stop on an airfield to repair and rearm · P / Esc = pause · N = mute
